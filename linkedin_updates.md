## 5. Networking tactic (the highest-leverage change)

Research on algorithmic hiring (Stanford, 2026) shows referrals can bypass automated
screening entirely, and that re-applying through the same vendor pipelines rarely produces
a new evaluation. So weight your effort toward warm paths:

- For each priority role, find one person to message before (or instead of) applying cold:
  a hiring manager, team lead, or recruiter for that role, or a University of Arizona alum
  at the company. Use your LinkedIn Premium InMail.
- Connect with weak ties on a rolling basis: UA alumni, former colleagues, people in
  healthcare-analytics and BI. Acquaintances surface more new opportunities than close ties.
- Lead every message and application with your portfolio link plus one specific, relevant
  project. Demonstrable work is what survives algorithmic filtering and what a referrer
  actually evaluates.
