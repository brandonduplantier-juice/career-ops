# Brandon Duplantier — STAR Story Bank

## Story 1: Organ Rejection Risk Scoring System
Situation: Patients recovering from organ transplants face rejection risk that
correlates directly with medication adherence. As a two-time kidney transplant
recipient myself, I understood this problem from lived experience and wanted to
model it computationally.
Task: Build a pipeline that could ingest raw dose records and classify patients
by rejection risk without manual review.
Action: Designed a weighted risk scoring formula combining missed doses, timing
errors, and late doses. Built the full pipeline in Python using CSV processing
and custom logic. Classified patients into three tiers and generated automated
clinical recommendations per tier. Structured output to be extensible to real
EHR formats.
Result: Working pipeline that processes patient records end-to-end and outputs
a structured summary CSV with risk scores and recommendations. Demonstrates
ability to translate clinical domain knowledge into a functional data system.

## Story 2: Amazon Sourcing Intelligence System
Situation: Manually reviewing hundreds of products for retail arbitrage
opportunities was too slow to be operationally useful.
Task: Build an automated system that could score and rank opportunities without
manual input.
Action: Connected Make.com, Airtable, and the Claude API into an end-to-end
pipeline. Designed scoring logic to filter signal from noise. Tuned API prompts
to return consistent structured JSON output. Validated results programmatically.
Result: Pipeline runs without manual input and outputs ranked actionable
opportunities. Reduced decision time from hours of manual review to automated
output.

## Story 3: Pathologist Assistant
Situation: Montgomery County Morgue needed part-time assistance with autopsy
procedures over a four-year period.
Task: Remove, document, and handle neurological and thoracic organs according
to strict anatomical protocols in a sterile environment.
Action: Performed consistent hands-on work alongside trained pathologists.
Maintained sterile protocols, documented findings precisely, and developed
firsthand understanding of human pathology and organ systems.
Result: Four years of reliable part-time service. Developed direct knowledge
of organ anatomy and pathology that directly informs my biomedical research
interests and my organ rejection risk scoring project.

## Story 5: False Memory Gaming Community
Situation: Online gaming communities typically collapse without structure.
Task: Build and sustain a 50+ member community with operational longevity.
Action: Designed hierarchy systems, SOPs, and a merit-based point system for
promotions. Hosted yearly retreats and monthly events. Built and maintained
