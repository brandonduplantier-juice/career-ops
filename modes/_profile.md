### Durability Filter (MANDATORY — apply before scoring)

Brandon has dependents and ongoing financial obligations. He needs roles that can last through December 2027 graduation and ideally convert to full-time with benefits after. Apply this filter to every role after the remote filter:

**Step 1 — Classify duration:**

| Signal in posting | Classification |
|-------------------|----------------|
| No end date mentioned; ongoing, permanent, or rolling | `✅ ONGOING` |
| Fixed end date OR named cohort (Summer 2026, Fall 2026, 12-week program, etc.) WITH language like "conversion", "path to full-time", "return offer", "renew", "long-term" | `⚠️ TEMPORARY/CONVERSION` |
| Fixed end date OR named cohort with NO conversion language | `🔴 TEMPORARY` |
| Multi-year grant-funded academic lab position; PI explicitly recruits long-term students | `✅ ONGOING` (treat same as ongoing) |

**Step 2 — Score adjustment:**

- `✅ ONGOING` → +0.4 score bonus
- `⚠️ TEMPORARY/CONVERSION` → no penalty, but cap at B score until conversion path is confirmed
- `🔴 TEMPORARY` → −0.5 score penalty; still include in queue but mark prominently; user makes final call
- Role end date falls before December 2027 with no conversion language → additional −0.5 (stacks with TEMPORARY penalty)
- Posting contains "post-graduation", "full-time offer", "convert to full-time", "return offer", or "path to permanent" → +0.4 bonus
- Posting contains "through graduation", "student-flexible", "flexible start/end", "work around your schedule", or "until graduation" → +0.3 bonus

**Step 3 — Prioritize by durability:**

Within the same letter grade, sort: ONGOING first, then TEMPORARY/CONVERSION, then TEMPORARY.

**What counts as "ongoing":**
- Junior analyst, research associate, or engineer roles with no stated end date at companies with 50+ employees and real revenue (not pre-product)
- Part-time or contractor roles explicitly open-ended or month-to-month
- Academic lab positions funded by multi-year grants (NIH R01, NSF, HHMI, etc.) where the lab has an ongoing student researcher program
- Any posting that says "through graduation", "flexible duration", "long-term preferred", or "part of the team"

**What counts as TEMPORARY:**
- Named program cohorts with fixed dates: "Summer 2026 Internship", "Fall 2026 Co-op", "12-week program", "10-week cohort"
- Any posting stating a specific end date without conversion language
- One-time project contracts with a defined deliverable and no renewal language
- AI trainer gigs (Mindrift, Alignerr, Outlier, Scale, DataAnnotation) — these are ongoing by nature (task-based) but income is not guaranteed; classify as `⚠️ TEMPORARY/CONVERSION` and note income uncertainty

**Exceptions — do NOT discard, but always flag:**
- Summer/fixed-term roles at elite research institutions (Broad, HHMI, NIH, Genentech, etc.) where the prestige or research experience significantly outweighs duration risk — include with `🔴 TEMPORARY` flag and a note on research value
- Any role explicitly mentioned by the user as a priority regardless of duration

### Company Stability Flag (apply alongside Durability Filter)

Flag every role with an employer stability tier. This is separate from duration — a STABLE company can still offer a temporary role, but the risk profile is different. Include the flag in the queue.

| Tier | Criteria | Flag |
|------|----------|------|
| **STABLE** | 200+ employees, real revenue, established product or service, publicly traded or well-funded Series C+, or government/federal agency | `🏢 STABLE` |
| **STARTUP** | Pre-revenue, seed/Series A/B, fewer than 200 employees, or YC-stage | `🚀 STARTUP` |
| **ACADEMIC** | University lab, research institute, non-profit research org | `🎓 ACADEMIC` |
| **GOV** | Federal or state government agency (NIH, CDC, USDA, state health dept) | `🏛️ GOV` |

**Score adjustment for stability:**
- `🏢 STABLE` + `✅ Ongoing` → +0.3 bonus (stable income through graduation)
- `🏛️ GOV` + `✅ Ongoing` → +0.4 bonus (federal benefits pathway, Pathways program converts to permanent)
- `🚀 STARTUP` + `🔴 Temporary` → no additional penalty (already penalized), but note funding stage
- `🎓 ACADEMIC` + multi-year grant → treat as `✅ Ongoing` regardless of the internship label

**When a posting is from a STABLE company:**
- State the employee count and revenue status in the Gap column if known
- Note explicitly if the company offers benefits (health insurance, 401k) — this matters for dependents
- Federal GOV roles: note if GS grade and Pathways-to-permanent conversion is available

### Dry Lab / Wet Lab Filter (academic and research lab roles)

For any role at a university, research institute, or non-profit research lab:
- Read the full job description for wet lab signals: pipetting, cell culture, PCR, animal handling, sample prep, bench work, microscopy operation (not image analysis), vivarium.
- If >20% of responsibilities are wet lab → mark `DISCARD: WET_LAB` and exclude from queue.
- If the role is purely computational (bioinformatics pipelines, data analysis, algorithm development, software engineering for biology) → include normally.
- Ambiguous mix with computational majority → include with note `⚠️ PARTIAL_WET_LAB`.
- This filter applies ONLY to academic/research institute roles, not to biotech/pharma companies where roles are generally dry unless specified.
